# 5. Echo Ranging

## Problem
A person shouts towards a cliff and hears the echo 1 seconds later. How far away is the cliff? (Speed of sound in air is 343 m/s).

## Solution

**Step 1 — Understand the path of the sound.**  
The sound travels from the person to the cliff, reflects, and comes back. If $d$ is the distance to the cliff, the **total distance** traveled by the sound is

$$D_{\text{total}} = 2d$$

**Step 2 — Apply distance = speed × time.**  

$$2d = v \cdot t$$

**Step 3 — Solve for $d$.**  

$$d = \frac{v \cdot t}{2}$$

**Step 4 — Plug in numerical values.**  

$$d = \frac{343\ \text{m/s} \times 1\ \text{s}}{2} = 171.5\ \text{m}$$

## Answer

$$\boxed{d = 171.5\ \text{m}}$$
