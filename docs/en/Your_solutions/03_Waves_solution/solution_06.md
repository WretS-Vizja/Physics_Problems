# 6. Wave Equation

## Problem
A wave is described by the equation $y(x,t) = 0.05 \sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)$, where x and y are in meters and t is in seconds. Determine the waves':

a) Amplitude $A$.  
b) Wavelength $\lambda$.  
c) Frequency $f$.  
d) Wave speed $v$.

## Solution

**Step 1 — Identify the standard form.**  
A traveling wave moving in the $+x$ direction has the general form

$$y(x,t) = A\sin(kx - \omega t)$$

Comparing with $y(x,t) = 0.05\sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)$:

- $A = 0.05$  
- $k = 2\pi\ \text{rad/m}$  
- $\omega = 50\pi\ \text{rad/s}$

### a) Amplitude

The amplitude is the coefficient in front of the sine:

$$A = 0.05\ \text{m}$$

### b) Wavelength

From $k = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}$:

$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k} = \frac{2\pi}{2\pi} = 1\ \text{m}$$

### c) Frequency

From $\omega = 2\pi f$:

$$f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi} = 25\ \text{Hz}$$

### d) Wave speed

Using $v = f\lambda$ (or equivalently $v = \omega/k$):

$$v = \frac{\omega}{k} = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi} = 25\ \text{m/s}$$

Check: $v = f\lambda = 25 \times 1 = 25\ \text{m/s}$. ✓

## Answer

$$\boxed{A = 0.05\ \text{m},\quad \lambda = 1\ \text{m},\quad f = 25\ \text{Hz},\quad v = 25\ \text{m/s}}$$
